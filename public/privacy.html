Front Desk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Front Desk is a voice-booking service for small local businesses (barbers, clinics, pet groomers, and similar). This page explains what information Front Desk collects when you book, order, or manage an appointment through it, and how that information is used.

What we collect

  • Name and phone number — collected when you book an appointment, place an order, or link your account, so a business can identify your booking and, if you've opted in, text you about it.
  • Appointment and order details — the service, business, staff member, time, and price of what you book or order.
  • Voice request content — the text of what you ask Alexa+ to do, as needed to fulfill that specific request (e.g. "book a haircut tomorrow afternoon").

SMS / text notifications

Front Desk never sends you a text message unless you've explicitly opted in by telling it to (for example, "yes, text me reminders"). You can opt out at any time by saying so — just as easily as opting in — and no further texts will be sent. A phone number being on file is never treated as consent on its own.

How information is used

Information you provide is used only to operate the booking/ordering flow you asked for: finding a business, checking availability, holding and confirming a slot or order, sending a reminder or order-ready notification (only with your consent), and letting the business owner see their own bookings on their dashboard. Front Desk does not sell your information, and does not use it for advertising.

Third parties

Front Desk may use Amazon Bedrock on the business-owner side only (for turning a plain-language business description into a structured catalog, and for a daily owner briefing) — this never involves customer-submitted booking data. If a business has configured real SMS delivery, a notification you've opted into is sent through Twilio, a third-party messaging provider, solely to deliver that message to your phone.

Questions

This is a hackathon project. For questions about this policy, contact the project maintainer listed in the add-on's developer information.
