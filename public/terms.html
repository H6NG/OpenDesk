Last updated: October 2026

Front Desk is a voice-booking add-on that connects customers to small local businesses through Alexa+. By using Front Desk, you agree to these terms.

What Front Desk does

Front Desk lets you find a participating business, check its availability, and hold, confirm, reschedule, or cancel an appointment or item order by voice. It is a booking tool, not the business itself — Front Desk relays your request to the business's own schedule and does not independently verify the business's services, pricing, hours, or policies beyond what the business has entered.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information (name, phone number) when booking or ordering, so the business can identify and honor your request.
  • Follow the cancellation/no-show policy disclosed to you by the business at booking time — late cancellation fees, where they apply, are set and collected by the business, not by Front Desk.
  • Only opt into text notifications if you actually want them, and understand you can opt out at any time.

No guarantee of availability

A held slot or order is not final until confirmed, and holds expire after a short window. Front Desk makes a good-faith effort to prevent double-booking and to accurately reflect stock, but cannot guarantee a business's real-world capacity, staffing, or inventory beyond what's recorded in its system at the moment of your request.
